Project overview

Artificial Intelligence is reshaping society quickly. To understand where it is heading and what footprint it leaves, we need to look at the data.

This project turns abstract numbers into visual narratives. Each visualization examines a different side of AI's impact:

  • How AI is changing economic landscapes
  • How AI research and development is growing and spreading
  • How companies and countries adopt AI technology

The website grows with the course: roughly every week we add a secondary page that explores the data with a different visualization type.

Data sources

Our analyses rely on five authoritative platforms.

  • Stanford AI Index Report (2026) Comprehensive metrics on global AI research and development.
  • Our World in Data: Artificial Intelligence Societal impacts and long-term AI trends.
  • Eurostat AI adoption and usage in European enterprises.
  • Anthropic Economic Index (Hugging Face) Economic data on how AI is used across occupations and tasks.
  • OECD.AI Policy observatory data on global AI investment and incidents.
